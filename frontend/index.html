About CafeBot

CafeBot started as a small counter with one espresso machine and a big idea: coffee should bring people together. Today we roast in small batches, bake daily, and source ingredients from local partners we trust. Whether you're grabbing a quick espresso or settling in for the afternoon, our doors — and our seats — are open.
